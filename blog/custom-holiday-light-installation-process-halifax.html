Custom Holiday Light Installation in Halifax: How It Works, Start to Finish

By Kendall Scott October 31, 2026 7 min read

Quick answer

A custom holiday light install with WashCrafters runs from a design walk-through to a January takedown, and we handle everything in between. We design the look with you, build the lights to fit your home, install them with clips that leave nothing behind, fix anything that goes dark all season, then take it all down and store it for next year. You choose the bulbs, the colours and the extras.

Most people picture holiday lighting as a box of tangled strands and a long afternoon on a ladder. Doing it professionally is a different job, and a lot of what makes a display look good happens before anyone leaves the ground. Here's how it works at WashCrafters, from the first walk-through to the day your set goes into storage, and how much of it is up to you.

Why Every Set Is Custom-Cut to Your Home

A store-bought strand comes in a fixed length. Your house doesn't. That mismatch is where the gaps, the droop and the extra cord dangling off the end come from. A strand that's too short stops before the roofline does, and one that's too long gets bunched up or draped where it shouldn't be.

We build your lights to your home's measurements, run by run, so they start and finish where your roofline does. Peaks, dormers, eaves, columns and windows are all cut to fit. It's the biggest single reason a professional display looks clean from the street, and it's why the lights, clips and timer are all ours. Everything is commercial-grade LED, chosen for the job.

The Process, Step by Step

1The design walk-through

We start with your house, not a price list. We either walk the property with you or look over a few photos you send, and we map out the look together: which rooflines, peaks and windows to light, what colour, which bulb style, and any extras. You get a firm quote from that, so there are no surprises on install day.

2Your lights are made to fit

Once the design is settled, your lights are prepared to the plan. Each run is cut to the length your house needs, in the bulb style and colour chosen for that part of the design.

3Install day

A fully insured, ladder-trained crew puts it all up, usually in a single visit. Most homes are done in a few hours. Lights are attached with plastic clips designed for the job, never staples, nails or adhesive, so your gutters, shingles and trim aren't damaged, and the clips come off cleanly at takedown. Cords are routed out of sight instead of across the lawn, and we set the timer so everything comes on at dusk by itself.

4Enjoy it, and we maintain it

Wind, ice and the odd squirrel happen. If a section goes dark or a clip lets go, text us and we'll come put it right at no charge. Free service calls run all season, so nobody in your family needs to climb a frosty ladder in December.

5Takedown in January

Everything comes down in January, weather permitting. A rough winter can push it into early February. Nothing is left behind on your roofline.

6Storage until next year

We store your set between seasons, labelled, so next year's install goes up faster. Because your set is already built and stored, returning customers are quoted a lower re-install price, and we reach out in early fall to lock in your spot before the rush.

What Sets a Professional Install Apart

Height and December frost are a bad combination. Our crew is fully insured and ladder-trained, and we work on roofs and gutters across the HRM all year, so your roofline isn't unfamiliar ground. Because it's one crew doing the whole job in one visit, the roofline, columns, wreaths and trees come out coordinated instead of looking like a patchwork.

It's also the small things. Hidden cords, clips that don't mark anything, a timer that's already set, and a number to text if something goes out. If your gutters are full, it's worth clearing them before the lights go up, since that's where much of the display attaches. We covered the timing in how often to clean your gutters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the lights really custom-cut to my home?

Yes. Every run is built to your home's measurements, so the lights start and finish where your roofline, peaks, columns and windows do. There are no store-bought strands that are too short or too long, and no loose cord hanging off the end.

What bulb styles can I choose?

C9, C7, G30 and G50 globes, and 5 mm mini lights for window frames, railings, trees and shrubs, in warm white, classic multi-colour or something bolder. You can add pre-lit wreaths, garland, wrapped columns and trees, window outlines, pathway stakes and lawn accents. We'll recommend what suits your home.

Do I own the lights?

No. The lights, clips and timer stay with WashCrafters, and we store them for you between seasons. You're paying for the design, installation, in-season service and takedown, and you don't end up with a box of lights in your basement.

What happens if a section goes out?

Text us and we'll come put it right at no charge. Free service calls are included all season, so a gust of wind or a patch of ice doesn't leave you with a dark spot on the roofline.

Will the lights damage my gutters or shingles?

No. We attach everything with plastic clips designed for the job, never staples, nails or adhesive. They come off cleanly at takedown and leave nothing behind.

When do you take the lights down?

In January, weather permitting. A rough winter can push takedown into early February. Everything comes down in one go, and your set goes into storage until next season.
