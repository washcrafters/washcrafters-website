Those dark streaks running down your roof aren't dirt or age — they're a living cyanobacteria called Gloeocapsa magma, feeding on the limestone filler in your shingles. Halifax's humidity, salt air and freeze-thaw cycles make the HRM close to a perfect growing environment for it, which is why the problem shows up here more than in most other parts of the country.

It's more than cosmetic. The bacteria holds moisture against your shingles, which speeds up the loss of the granules that protect them from UV damage — quietly shortening your roof's lifespan. We treat it with a low-pressure application that kills the growth at the source, so it rinses away naturally over the following weeks instead of stripping granules the way a pressure washer would.

Questions

Roof Cleaning FAQ

How does roof washing benefit my home?

Beyond curb appeal, it removes the biological growth that traps moisture against your shingles and shortens their lifespan — a modest cleaning now can delay a roof replacement conversation for years.

Is roof cleaning safe for my shingles?

Yes, when it's done the right way. We use low-pressure application, never a pressure washer directly on shingles, which is what causes granule loss and voided warranties.

How often should I have my roof cleaned?

Every 2–3 years is typical in the HRM given our climate, though homes on shaded or north-facing lots may need it sooner.

Do you use eco-friendly cleaning products?

Yes, and we pre-rinse and protect landscaping below the roofline before we start.

Can you remove moss from my roof?

Yes — moss and lichen respond well to the same low-pressure treatment we use for algae streaking.
