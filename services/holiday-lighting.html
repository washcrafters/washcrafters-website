Questions

Holiday Lighting FAQ

Do I own the lights?

No — the lights, clips and timer stay with WashCrafters and we store them for you between seasons. You're paying for the design, the install, in-season service and takedown. It keeps your cost down and means there's no box of lights taking up your basement.

What if a section goes out mid-season?

Text us and we'll come put it right at no charge. Commercial LED is reliable, but wind, ice and the odd squirrel happen — that's what the free service calls are for.

When do you install and take down?

Installs run from late October through early December. Booking by October 31 gets you 15% off and your choice of week. Takedown is in January, weather permitting — sometimes into early February if it's a rough one.

Can you hang lights I already own?

Our packages use our commercial-grade LED so we can stand behind the look and the service. If you have a specific set you love, ask us — we can sometimes work it in, though we can't warranty bulbs we didn't supply.

Will this damage my gutters or shingles?

No. We attach with plastic clips designed for the job — never staples, nails or adhesive. Clips come off cleanly at takedown and leave nothing behind.

Warm white or colour — which should I pick?

Warm white is the safe, elegant choice and suits almost every home. Classic multi-colour is great if you want that nostalgic feel — we keep it to the traditional C9 palette so it still looks considered, not chaotic.

Does it run up my power bill?

Barely. LED holiday lights draw a fraction of old incandescent strings, and the timer only runs them in the evening. A full roofline typically costs a few dollars over the whole season.

Do you cover my area?

Yes — holiday lighting is offered across the entire Halifax Regional Municipality: Halifax, Dartmouth, Bedford, Sackville, Hammonds Plains, Fall River, Cole Harbour, Eastern Passage, Timberlea and everywhere in between.
