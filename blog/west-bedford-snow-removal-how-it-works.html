West Bedford Snow Removal: How Our Winter Service Works

By Kendall Scott November 6, 2026 7 min read

Quick answer

We clear a limited number of driveways in Stonington Park and West Bedford, on foot with a commercial snowblower and shovel, for a flat seasonal price that runs from November to April. You get a text when we're on our way and another when it's cleared. After overnight snow, your driveway and walkway are done before 7:30 a.m. on weekdays, and there's no cap on the number of visits.

If you've ever stood at the window at 6:30 in the morning, watching the snow pile up and wondering when anyone is coming, this one is for you. Here's how our West Bedford snow removal works, from signing up to the "cleared" text, and what you actually get for your money.

Why We Only Serve West Bedford

WashCrafters is a West Bedford business, and this is the one service we keep deliberately local. Snow doesn't wait, and a driveway two streets over gets cleared a lot faster than one on the far side of Bedford. So each winter we take on a limited number of driveways inside the Stonington Park pocket and run that route hard every storm. Stretch the map and promises like "done before you leave for work" stop being real. When the route is full, we start a waitlist instead.

Why We Clear on Foot, Not With a Plow Truck

We clear with a commercial snowblower and shovels, on foot. That's a deliberate choice. There are no tire ruts across your lawn, no plow blade catching your interlock or paving stones, and no wall of packed snow shoved across your garage door. It also lets us get in close to the door, the walkway and the oil fill, which a truck can't do.

How It Works, Step by Step

1Reserve your spot

Send us your address and a photo of the driveway. If you'd like to check that you're in the service area first, text your address to (902) 333-9929 and we'll confirm within the hour. We confirm your plan and price, usually the same day.

2We map your property

Before the first snow, we note where snow can be piled, where your markers are, and any tight spots. We agree with you on the most out-of-the-way place to pile it, so nothing is a surprise in January.

3It snows, and we go

You get an "on our way" text when we head out. We clear the driveway, plus the walkway, steps and ice melt if your plan includes them, and then you get a "cleared" text when it's done. No wondering, no phone tag.

4Your driveway is cleared on schedule

When snow ends overnight, your driveway and walkway are done before 7:30 a.m. on weekday mornings. For daytime or ongoing storms, we clear within about 6 hours of the snow stopping, and we make an earlier pass on long storms so it never gets away from us.

5Repeat all winter

You don't need to call before each storm. We come every time it snows enough, at one flat seasonal price, right through to spring.

The "starting at" rates cover a typical West Bedford two-car driveway. Your final price depends on the size, slope and layout of your driveway. Existing WashCrafters cleaning customers take 10% off any snow removal plan.

Season and Billing

Seasons run November 1 to April 30. Seasonal plans are billed in two instalments, the first when you sign up and the second on January 15, and you can pay by e-transfer or card. Seasonal customers are always cleared first.

Prefer to Pay Per Storm?

When the route has room, we also take on-call clearing: $99 for a single or compact driveway, $129 for a standard two-car, and $169 for a large or multi-car driveway. A walkway add-on is $45.

What About the Ridge the City Plow Leaves?

Driveway clearing covers your driveway itself. The ridge of packed snow the city plow leaves at the end of it is a separate, heavier job that depends on when the plow comes through relative to our visit. If you end up with one, text us and we'll come clear it as an add-on.

Where We Clear

Our winter route covers the Stonington Park pocket of West Bedford, roughly bounded by Hammonds Plains Road to the north, Highway 102 to the east, and Larry Uteck Boulevard to the south-west. That includes Broad Street, Gary Martin Drive, Hollyhock Way, Capstone Crescent, Larkview Terrace and Lasalle Court, and the streets running off them. Boundaries are approximate, so if you're near the edge, ask and we'll tell you straight. You can see the full map on our West Bedford snow removal page.

A Few Things That Make It Easier

If you can, keep the driveway clear of cars on storm nights, and mark the edges of the driveway and anything that hides under snow, such as a planter bed, a step or a curb corner. Tell us about anything we should know. It helps us do a cleaner job from the first storm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit on visits in a bad winter?

No. Seasonal plans are a flat price no matter how many times it snows, so a brutal February costs the same as a quiet one. There are no per-visit charges and no surge pricing for big storms.

Do you use a plow truck?

No. We clear with a commercial snowblower and shovels, on foot. That means no tire ruts on your lawn, no plow blade catching your interlock or paving stones, and no wall of packed snow pushed across your garage door. We can also get in tight to the door, the walkway and the oil fill.

How much snow triggers a visit?

On the Driveway and Driveway + Walkway plans, we clear after any snowfall of 5 cm or more. Full Winter Care has no minimum: we clear every snowfall, and we salt the driveway ahead of freezing rain.

When will my driveway actually be cleared?

When snow ends overnight, your driveway and walkway are done before 7:30 a.m. on weekday mornings. For daytime or ongoing storms, we clear within about 6 hours of the snow stopping, and we make an earlier pass on long storms so it never gets away from us.

Do you clear the ridge the city plow leaves at the end of my driveway?

Not automatically. That ridge depends on when the city plow comes through relative to our visit, so it isn't part of standard driveway clearing. If one is left behind, text us and we'll come clear it as an add-on.

What if I sign up mid-season?

If there's room on the route, we prorate the season from your start date. If the route is full, we add you to the waitlist and take per-storm bookings when we can fit them in.
