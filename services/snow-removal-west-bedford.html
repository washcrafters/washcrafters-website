Questions

West Bedford Snow Removal FAQ

Why only West Bedford?

Because being close is the whole point. A tight service area is how we can promise your driveway is done before you leave in the morning and get to it fast when a storm rolls through. Stretch the map and those promises stop being real.

Do you use a plow truck?

No — we clear with a commercial snowblower and shovels, on foot. That means no tire ruts on your lawn, no plow blade catching your interlock or paving stones, and no wall of packed snow shoved across your garage door. We can also get in tight to the door, the walkway and the oil fill, which a truck can't.

How much snow triggers a visit?

On the Driveway and Driveway + Walkway plans we clear at 5 cm or more. Full Winter Care has no minimum — we clear every snowfall, plus proactive salting before freezing rain.

When will my driveway actually be cleared?

For snow that ends overnight, your driveway and walkway are done before 7:30 a.m. on weekday mornings. For daytime or ongoing storms, we clear within about 6 hours of the snow stopping, and we'll make an earlier pass on long storms so it never gets away on us.

Do you clear the ridge the city plow leaves at the end of my driveway?

Yes. That windrow is included on every plan — it's the part most people care about most.

Is there a limit on the number of visits in a bad winter?

No. Seasonal plans are a flat price no matter how many times it snows. A brutal February costs you the same as a quiet one.

Where does the snow go?

We pile it in the most out-of-the-way spot on your property — we agree on that before the season starts. If your lot fills up mid-winter, we'll talk about hauling options.

How do I pay?

Seasonal plans are split into two instalments: the first when you sign up, the second on January 15. E-transfer or card.

What if I sign up mid-season?

If there's room on the route we'll prorate the season from your start date. If it's full, we'll add you to the waitlist and take per-storm bookings when we can fit them in.
